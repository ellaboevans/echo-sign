ISSUE IDENTIFIED:
  Dropdown menu in spaces page had hardcoded styling that was breaking
  design system compliance and making the menu appear incorrectly.

LOCATION:
  File: app/dashboard/spaces/page.tsx
  Lines: 196-199 (before) → 196-197 (after)

ROOT CAUSE:
  Custom className on DropdownMenuContent was overriding design system:
  
  className="bg-white shadow-xl text-black border-black/30 border w-60"
  
  This hardcoded styling was forcing:
    - White background (instead of bg-popover)
    - Black text (instead of text-popover-foreground)
    - Custom black border (instead of design system ring)
    - Fixed width (instead of responsive)
    - Custom shadow (instead of design system shadow-md)

SOLUTION APPLIED:
  Removed all custom className from DropdownMenuContent
  
  Before:
    <DropdownMenuContent
      className="bg-white shadow-xl text-black border-black/30 border w-60"
      align="end">
  
  After:
    <DropdownMenuContent
      align="end">

DESIGN SYSTEM STYLING NOW APPLIED:
  Background: bg-popover (design token)
  Text: text-popover-foreground (design token)
  Border: ring-1 ring-foreground/10 (design system)
  Shadow: shadow-md (design system)
  Width: min-w-32 (responsive, design system)
  Animations: data-open:animate-in (design system)
  Radius: rounded-none (design system)
